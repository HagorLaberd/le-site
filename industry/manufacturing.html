Industrials & manufacturing

Technology and automation are empowering industrial and manufacturing organizations to transform their operations and supply chains to strengthen their competitive advantage and enhance agility. Meanwhile, leaders of these organizations navigate constant challenges related to input costs, geopolitical forces, competitive threats, and workforce dynamics; all while innovating and creating value for customers.

How we help

 

Optimize business operations

Whether you are innovating your business model, modernizing technology systems, or transforming the way work gets done, we use our deep industry, functional, and technical expertise to help organizations develop the right strategies, align resources, and utilize technologies and data to manage costs, optimize investments and drive growth. Learn more.

Transform customer experience

From developing new services to adopting technology that unlocks real-time customer insights, we work with you to create a sustainable competitive advantage.

Accelerate digital transformation

We develop the strategies and vet, implement, and maintain the systems that equip your team to meet and surpass growth goals. Learn more.

Strategize and innovate for the future

We empower you to think differently to build innovation capabilities, optimize business performance, and create new growth strategies that will help you proactively address complex business challenges and stay ahead of market disruption. Learn more.

Foster a future-ready workforce

Our combination of strategy, deep expertise, and creativity can help you enable your team to achieve your strategic transformation.

Scale and streamline with outsourced support

Our managed services team helps you shift administrative, financial, technical, and regulatory burden from your teams so they can focus more time on key strategic initiatives.

Navigate complex financial transactions

Whether an M&A deal, a critical capital expenditure, or a financing strategy, our team has the operational and financial acumen to help any organization make strategic, data-driven decisions to optimize stakeholder buy-in, cash flow, return on investment, and organizational resilience.

Manage through distress successfully

Our restructuring and turnaround experts advise organizations through disparate stakeholder negotiations, robust liquidity management and enhancement, in- and out-of-court restructuring, dispute advisory and litigation, and operational improvements to create a viable path forward for stakeholders.

Manage your real estate portfolio

Huron’s team of experienced real estate professionals works with clients to optimize their real estate portfolios, applying skilled, proven strategies to maximize cost efficiencies and unlock significant property value.Learn more. 





Optimize your supply chain

Enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and stay resilient with expert strategies for supply chain optimization. Whatever your current challenges, our team can help you tackle disruption and drive recovery with confidence.

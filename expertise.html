How I Help

A heritage of healers and Quranic masters

Cheikh Mohamed Hamin Haidra did not learn from books alone. He received transmission from father to son, from master to disciple. His lineage goes back several generations of traditional African healers and Quranic masters. Men who knew how to see the invisible, break curses, protect the innocent, and heal through plants and prayers. This transmission is not theoretical. 40 years helping those who suffer in silence

For over four decades, Cheikh Mohamed Hamin Haidra has welcomed people from all walks of life. Betrayed women crying in silence. Broken men crushed by failure. 🔍

Diagnosis first, then action with discretion

The sheikh never rushes. Before any intervention, he takes the time to sit with you. He listens to you. He asks you questions. He seeks to understand what is wrong. Then he prays. A prayer of consultation, istikhara. He asks Allah and his ancestors to reveal the truth to him. Next, he verifies. Is it envy? A curse cast by an enemy? A spirit inherited from your ancestors? Or none of the above? When he has the answer, he acts. With a solution adapted to your situation. A distant ritual, or on-site if needed. And no one will know. Discretion is paramount.

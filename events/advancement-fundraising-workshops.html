Learn best-practice skills, tools, and strategies to level up your fundraising with live, research-based public workshops for advancement teams and nonprofit leaders.

These sessions are open to anyone engaged in advancement and fundraising, with virtual and in-person options available throughout the year.

Our comprehensive catalog includes workshops tailored to different roles and levels, with tracks supporting development professionals, deans and academic leaders, healthcare professionals, and nonprofit executives.

 

Elevate your expertise. Amplify your impact.

  • Proven, donor-centric frameworks. Put what you learn to work immediately with tools that are grounded in decades of fundraising research and field application, such as the Donor Commitment Continuum and Priority Grid.
  • Live and interactive experiences. Benefit from facilitator-led learning opportunities designed for participation and immediate application, including breakouts, practice, and coaching.
  • CFRE credits. Nearly all of our workshops qualify for continuing education credits.

Workshops - Register today

 

Key Concepts for Donor Development

Diagnose commitment, unlock philanthropic passion with a guided question process, and chart next steps toward meaningful, multiyear support.

Best for: Early to mid-career fundraisers who want a high-impact fundamentals refresher

Virtual, Mar. 10–11, 2026

 
The Art and Science of Donor Development

Enhance your understanding of the core donor development process, practice ask language, and learn how to align passions with mission using signature tools, including the Donor Commitment Continuum, Priority Grid, and more.

Best for: Major gifts officers, development leaders, and anyone moving into principal/major giving

Williamsburg, VA., Mar. 31 – April 1, 2026

Denver, CO., June 15–16, 2026

Virtual, July 20–24, 2026

Portland, OR., Oct. 13–14, 2026

Chicago, IL., Dec. 8–9, 2026

 

Professional Fundraising for Deans and Academic Leaders 

Develop high-functioning partnerships between academic leadership and advancement. Collaborate to create Opportunity Stories, align academic and philanthropic priorities, and set a plan to lead a culture for philanthropy.

Best for: Deans, department chairs, research leaders, and their development partners

Denver, CO., June 17–18, 2026

 

Campaigns of One: Inspiring Principal Giving

Discover a groundbreaking approach to engaging benefactors in co-creating transformational philanthropic opportunities. This highly interactive, virtual education series is designed for those who aspire to meaningfully engage, inspire, and collaborate with benefactors.

Best for: Development leaders, principal giving professionals, and experienced major giving professionals

Virtual, April 20–22, 2026

Denver, CO., Sept. 17–18, 2026

 

 

ASDD: Application Lab

The ASDD Application Lab is an exclusive, small-group learning experience designed limited to 12 participants. This workshop takes the core principles of The Art and Science of Donor Development and brings them to life through immersive donor role plays, peer collaboration, and expert facilitator coaching.

Best for: Graduates of ASDD

Chicago, IL., July 15–16, 2026

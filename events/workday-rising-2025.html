Join us at Workday Rising 2025


Huron returns to Workday Rising 2025 as a Gold Sponsor, reinforcing our commitment to helping organizations lead with AI-driven transformation across finance, HR, and IT. This premier event brings together Workday customers and ecosystem leaders to explore how AI and human-centric innovation are shaping the future of work.

From complex ERP and EPM implementations to long-term platform optimization, Huron is a top Workday implementation partner with proven ability to deliver results. We combine deep industry knowledge with strong digital execution to help clients apply AI in ways that improve decision-making, increase agility, and unlock new opportunities. Whether expanding your Workday footprint or recovering from a failed implementation, Huron equips you to build smarter systems and achieve real business outcomes.
